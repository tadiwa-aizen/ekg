PROBLEM
Despite their growing significance, current evaluation methods are often fragmented,
addressing only a subset of the features that define an EKG. For instance, existing methods
may focus on the accuracy of event representation or temporal relationships but fail to
provide a comprehensive assessment that considers all the critical dimensions of an EKG,
such as causal links, contextual information, and the ability to handle complex queries
involving multiple events.
